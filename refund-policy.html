Refund Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Because Soft Mack may provide customized digital services and digital products, refund eligibility can depend on the nature and stage of the work.

Custom services

For custom web design, development, consulting, marketing, creative work, or other services, approved deposits or payments may be non-refundable once work has started, except where a written agreement or applicable law provides otherwise.

Before work begins

If a cancellation request is made before substantive work begins, Soft Mack may consider a refund of eligible prepaid amounts, less any non-recoverable costs, third-party charges, or work already completed.

Digital products

Digital products may be non-refundable after delivery or access, except where required by applicable law or where a product-specific refund promise states otherwise.

Project cancellation

If a project is cancelled after work has started, the client may be responsible for fees corresponding to work completed and approved expenses incurred up to cancellation.

How to request a refund

Email softmack0@gmail.com with your name, order or project details, the date of purchase, and the reason for your request. We will review the request according to the applicable agreement and this policy.

Exceptions

Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived under applicable law.
